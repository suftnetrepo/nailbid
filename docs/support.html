NailBid

Support

NailBid is a quoting and invoicing app for tradespeople and small construction businesses — quotes, invoices, customers and VAT/CIS calculations, all handled locally on your device.

Contact us

For help with the app, a bug report, or a question about your NailBid Pro subscription, email support@suftnet.dev. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

  • Is my data backed up? No — everything you enter (customers, quotes, invoices) is stored only on your device. There's no cloud sync, so back up your device regularly if that data matters to you.
  • How do I remove the "Made with NailBid" watermark? Go to Settings → NailBid Pro and choose a plan. It applies immediately to every export from then on.
  • How do I restore a purchase after reinstalling? Open Settings → NailBid Pro and tap "Restore purchases".
  • How do I remove or change my PIN? Go to Settings → Security.
